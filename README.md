## 🤖 Responsible AI

I believe that Artificial Intelligence should be developed and used responsibly. These are the core principles I try to uphold:

| Principle | Description |
| --------- | ----------- |
| ⚖️ **Fairness** | AI systems should treat all people fairly and avoid affecting similarly situated groups of people in different ways. |
| 🔒 **Reliability & Safety** | AI systems should perform reliably and safely, behaving as designed and responding safely to unanticipated situations. |
| 🔏 **Privacy & Security** | AI systems should be secure and respect privacy, protecting personal data and preventing misuse. |
| 🌍 **Inclusiveness** | AI systems should empower everyone and engage people, serving diverse needs across society. |
| 💡 **Transparency** | AI systems should be understandable, with people able to know how they work and make informed decisions. |
| 🧑‍⚖️ **Accountability** | People should be accountable for AI systems, ensuring appropriate human oversight and control. |

> *"The goal of AI should be to augment human capabilities, not replace human judgment."*
